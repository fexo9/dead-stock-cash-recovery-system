## The scoring rules (the whole system in one table)

Enter cost, price, stock, and units sold in your analysis period. The
workbook computes:

- **Velocity** = units sold ÷ analysis days (default 90).
- **Sell-through %** = units sold ÷ (stock + units sold).
- **Days of cover** = stock ÷ velocity (99,999 = infinite, velocity is zero).
- **Trapped capital** = stock × cost price.
- **Status:** DEAD if velocity ≤ 0.20/day; SLOW if ≤ 0.70/day; else HEALTHY.

| Situation | First move |
|---|---|
| DEAD, margin ≥ 40%, cover ≤ 1 year (365 days) | Markdown 20–30% |
| DEAD, zero sales, or cover > 1 year | Liquidate or donate |
| DEAD, thin margin (< 40%) | Bundle or markdown 15–25% |
| SLOW | Re-merchandise / bundle |
| HEALTHY | Keep selling |

## One SKU, end to end (FICTIONAL data)

**Tray — cost $6.50, price $12.99, stock 40, sold 14 in 90 days.**

- Velocity: 14 ÷ 90 = **0.16/day** → DEAD (≤ 0.20).
- Sell-through: 14 ÷ (40 + 14) = **25.93%**.
- Cover: 40 ÷ 0.1556 = **257.14 days**.
- Margin: (12.99 − 6.50) ÷ 12.99 = **49.96%**.
- Trapped: 40 × 6.50 = **$260.00**.
- Action: DEAD + margin ≥ 40% + cover ≤ 365 → **Markdown 20–30%**.

Markdown math (minimum margin kept: 10%):

- Max-safe discount = 1 − 6.50 ÷ (12.99 × 0.90) = 1 − 6.50 ÷ 11.691 =
  **44.40%**.
- Sale price at max-safe = 12.99 × (1 − 0.4440) = **$7.22**.
- Profit per unit = 7.22 − 6.50 = **$0.72** (margin 10.0% — exactly target).
- Estimated cash if all 40 sell at max-safe = 7.22 × 40 = **$288.89**.

And the ladder warns you: at 50% off, this tray's profit per unit is
12.99 × 0.50 − 6.50 = 6.495 − 6.50 = **−$0.01** — a loss. The full
workbook shows the 10/20/30/40/50% ladder for every SKU so you see that
before you print the sign.

## First 3 steps (from the Quick-Start Guide)

1. **Setup (2 min):** confirm the analysis period (default 90 days), the
   DEAD/SLOW thresholds (0.20 / 0.70), and the minimum margin (10%). Leave
   the defaults alone on your first run.
2. **Score (8 min):** on the Velocity Scorer, enter your suspect SKUs
   (SKU, name, category, cost, price, stock, units sold). Sort by trapped
   capital, descending — your top 5 rows are where the money is.
3. **Price the markdowns (3 min):** on the Markdown Optimizer, type each
   markdown candidate's SKU and read the max-safe discount, sale price,
   profit per unit, and verdict. Never discount deeper than max-safe without
   knowingly accepting the loss.
